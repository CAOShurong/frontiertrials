# Code of Conduct

## Commitment

We commit to a respectful, harassment-free environment regardless of identity, experience,
background, or point of view.

## Expected conduct

- Critique evidence, protocols, software, and conclusions rather than people.
- Distinguish observed interface behavior from claims about a provider or model.
- State uncertainty and disclose material evaluation limitations.
- Respect confidentiality, intellectual property, subscription terms, and research participants.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, sexualized conduct, deliberate misrepresentation,
or publishing confidential prompts and ballots is not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the maintainer through the repository's security contact. Content may
be edited or removed and participation may be restricted. Reports will be handled as confidentially
as practical.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
