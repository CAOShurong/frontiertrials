Methods

A chain of custody for human evaluation.

Every transition creates a file that another investigator can inspect, reproduce, or challenge.

  1. 01

    Pre-specify

    Define tasks, rubric, exclusions, capture settings, and the interpretation boundary.

  2. 02

    Capture

    Store exact UTF-8 outputs with observed metadata and SHA-256 digests.

  3. 03

    Blind

    Create deterministic aliases, all-pairs comparisons, balanced sides, and reviewer assignments.

  4. 04

    Judge offline

    Collect scores, pairwise choice, confidence, flags, and rationale in self-contained packets.

  5. 05

    Adjudicate

    Review disagreement, low confidence, abstentions, and flags without exposing identities.

  6. 06

    Reveal and test

    Require assignment completion, then estimate ranking, uncertainty, and panel sensitivity.

Interpretation boundary

This trial is not a universal leaderboard.

Observed interface, not certified backend. A displayed label does not prove internal routing.

Preference, not truth. Human votes do not establish factual correctness, safety, or general intelligence.

Local evidence, not timeless behavior. Web products and tool policies can change without notice.

Sensitivity, not reviewer grading. Panel diagnostics describe the sample and do not identify a “bad” rater.
